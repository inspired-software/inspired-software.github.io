About

Founded to build the things we actually wanted.

Inspired Software, LLC is a small studio making native software for Apple platforms. It exists to pursue passion projects: the ones worth years of evenings, whether or not anybody spotted a market for them first.

Each app is written for the device it runs on, and behaves the way the rest of that device does.
